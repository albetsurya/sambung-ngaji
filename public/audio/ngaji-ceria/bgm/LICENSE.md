# Ngaji Ceria background music

`menu.ogg`, `quiz.ogg`, and `battle.ogg` are original procedural compositions
generated by `scripts/generate-ngaji-bgm.py`. They use synthesized marimba-style
tones, with no recordings or third-party samples.

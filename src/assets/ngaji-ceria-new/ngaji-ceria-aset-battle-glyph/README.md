## Pemetaan pengganti
Karakter (BattleSetupFlow / BattleSetupPanel / QuizBattleArena)
- adventurer-boy: avatarBoy (potret bulat), avatarBoyBattle (badan penuh)
- qirra: avatarQirra, avatarQirraBattle
- qirra-safa: avatarSafa, avatarSafaBattle
- Musuh: monsterSlime, monsterGolem, monsterPixel (ganti 🟢 🪨 👾)

Glyph UI
- centang: iconCheckMark | gunting: iconScissors | gear: iconGearSettings | undo/redo: iconUndo, iconRedo | merge: iconMergeClips
- playhead (triangle dan bulat): iconPlayHeadMarker | play: iconPlay | stop: iconStop
- energi: iconEnergyPetir | slowmo: iconPowerSlowmo | perisai: iconPowerShield

Varian
- radar: iconRadarScanner | detektif: iconDetektifKacaPembesar | alkimia: iconAlkimiaTabung
- Wordle: tileWordleCorrect (hijau + centang), tileWordlePresent (kuning + lingkaran), tileWordleAbsent (abu + garis), tileWordleEmpty (kosong putus-putus).
  Tanda kecil di pojok membantu pemain buta warna; hapus jika tidak diperlukan.
